Mapping the market's blind spots.

Astrolab Intelligence runs autonomous research agents that turn scattered supply-chain and market data into a clear picture of where opportunity is going unclaimed.

What we do

Every supply chain leaves a trail: customs filings, supplier directories, shipping manifests, trade reporting, industry data. Most of it is public, and almost none of it gets read together.

Our research agents work through it continuously, checking demand against capacity, to find where a market is under-served. What comes out the other end is not a pile of raw data, but a specific opportunity a business can act on, drawn from a knowledge base that keeps growing with every engagement.

Capabilities
Autonomous research agents
Software agents continuously monitor public filings, trade data, supplier directories, and industry reporting, replacing weeks of manual desk research with an always-current view of a market.
Supply-chain gap detection
Demand signals are checked against supplier capacity and logistics data to surface where a supply chain is stretched thin, exposed, or missing a player entirely.
Opportunity mapping
Findings become a ranked, sized set of opportunities specific to a client's footprint and strategy, not a generic market report.
A compounding knowledge base
Every engagement adds structured findings to a shared knowledge base, so the next research effort starts from what is already known.
